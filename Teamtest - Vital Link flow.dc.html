WHAT THE SERVER OWNS

Matchmaking pool, room codes and invite links, the authoritative life meters, task assignment and seeds, and a 20 Hz presence channel carrying each player's touch position and tap events. The client renders; it never decides who lives.

Every line above is a real frame going through TeamTestNet. It runs in mock mode with no server; set the serverUrl tweak to a WebSocket and the same frames go over the wire. TeamTestNet.download() dumps the session as NDJSON. Contract and a starter Node server are in PROTOCOL.md.

PRESENCE ON TOUCH

Each teammate owns one screen edge. Their meter is that edge, their finger is the bead sliding along it, and every tap they make throws a ripple and leaves a fading mark. You never see their board, only that they are moving.
